Hispeedcity Champions Smart Mobility Revolution at Nikkei Forum Medini 2025 as Key Sponsor

MEDINI, JOHOR – June 30, 2025 – Hispeedcity Sdn Bhd, a pioneering Malaysian smart mobility solutions provider, solidified its commitment to disruptive digital innovation as an official sponsor of the prestigious Nikkei Forum Medini Johor 2025. The event, held at Sunway Big Box on June 18th & 19th, convened Asian industry leaders, government officials, and investors to explore the future of regional development.

The forum's agenda centered on critical drivers of progress: Digital Transformation, Sustainable ESG & Net-Zero Carbon initiatives, Regional Collaboration, and Smart City Development. These pillars resonate deeply with Hispeedcity's core mission: revolutionizing urban ecosystems through IR4.0 technologies (IoT + AI), starting with its foundational IoT-Smart Parking solutions to enable cashless, touch-less experiences and fast-flow traffic.

Hispeedcity's participation showcased its evolution beyond parking, demonstrating a comprehensive vision for smart mobility and city infrastructure. The company highlighted its technical expertise to deliver end-to-end projects, including:

➤ Smart Toll Systems for efficient highway management.
➤ Smart Traffic Solutions optimizing flow in CBDs.
➤ Smart Tourism enabled by frictionless "Scan & Go" experiences.
➤ Smart Subsidy Distribution systems supporting government initiatives.

"This forum was the perfect platform to demonstrate how Hispeedcity is powering Malaysia and Johor's ascent as a regional innovation hub," said a company spokesperson. "We engaged deeply with government delegates, Japanese and ASEAN investors, and industry peers during exhibitions and networking, showcasing our disruptive approach to smart mobility and digital city solutions."

Hispeedcity's sponsorship and active involvement positioned it as a key driver in the regional smart city dialogue. The forum successfully generated strategic opportunities to expand collaborations with local authorities, public hospitals, property developers, and highway operators seeking next-generation, integrated smart mobility digital solutions.

About Hispeedcity Sdn Bhd

Hispeedcity Sdn Bhd is a leading Malaysian technology innovator at the forefront of the smart city revolution. Leveraging Industry 4.0 technologies (IoT, AI, LPR), we design and implement integrated solutions that transform urban mobility and infrastructure. Starting with our core IoT-Smart Parking platform enabling cashless, touch-less transactions and traffic optimization, our expertise extends to Smart Tolling, Smart Traffic Management, Smart Tourism access, and Smart Subsidy systems, driving efficiency, sustainability, and enhanced citizen experiences.

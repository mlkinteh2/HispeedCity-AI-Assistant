Taipei Smart City Summit & Expo 2026

Hispeedcity at Taipei Smart City Summit & Expo 2026

Taipei, March 17, 2026 – Taiwan's Smart City Summit & Expo officially kicked off, bringing together global leaders in smart city innovation and digital transformation.

Hispeedcity proudly participated in this prestigious event, designed to highlight Taiwan's technological "soft power." The expo featured more than 2,250 booths and over 70 professional forums.

The event was organized by the Taipei Computer Association (TCA) and the National Development Council, attracting representatives from 174 cities across 53 countries.

During the event, Hispeedcity engaged with international delegates and city leaders, including representatives from São Paulo, Africa, Vietnam, Belize, and Taiwan officials.

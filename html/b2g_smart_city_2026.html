HISPEEDCITY Joins National B2G Smart City Initiative

Accelerating Malaysia’s Smart Nation Vision 2040

HISPEEDCITY has been officially invited by the Ministry of Housing and Local Government (KPKT) and PLANMalaysia to take part in the Business-to-Government (B2G) Smart City Incentive Programme 2026, a key national initiative supporting Malaysia’s ambition to achieve Smart Nation status by 2040.

Organized by PLANMalaysia, the session brought together more than 30 mayors and local authority heads from across Malaysia, alongside nearly 30 leading Industry 4.0, digital innovation and smart solution providers nationwide.

As an active participant, HISPEEDCITY presented its advanced smart mobility and smart parking solutions to support local governments in enhancing urban efficiency, connectivity and service quality.

This strategic public-private partnership platform enables local authorities to access expert technical advice, latest smart technologies and competitive solutions from certified providers.

HISPEEDCITY reaffirms its commitment to supporting Malaysia’s smart city agenda, working closely with all levels of government to build safer, smarter, more sustainable and liveable cities for all Malaysians.

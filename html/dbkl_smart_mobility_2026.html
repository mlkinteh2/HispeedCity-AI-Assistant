HISPEEDCITY Invited by DBKL for Smart Mobility KL CBD 2030

Kuala Lumpur Smart Mobility Initiative 2030

Kuala Lumpur, 9 February 2026 – HISPEEDCITY Sdn Bhd was honoured to be invited by Dewan Bandaraya Kuala Lumpur (DBKL) to present its digital solution on Smart Mobility, in support of preparations towards the 2030 Traffic Control and Management plan for Kuala Lumpur's Central Business District (CBD).

The session was chaired by a representative from Jabatan Undang-Undang dan Pendakwaan (JUUP) and attended by officers from Jabatan Perancangan Infrastruktur (JPIF), Jabatan Undang-Undang dan Pendakwaan (JUUP), and Jabatan Kejuruteraan Awam dan Saliran (JKAWS) of DBKL.

During the session, HISPEEDCITY presented its comprehensive end-to-end Smart Mobility and Smart Toll solution, designed to enhance traffic efficiency, reduce congestion, and strengthen digital traffic monitoring and enforcement within the CBD area.

The engagement provided an opportunity for constructive discussion on how innovative digital infrastructure can contribute to Kuala Lumpur's smart city transformation agenda leading up to 2030.

HISPEEDCITY remains fully prepared to support and collaborate with DBKL in implementing smart mobility initiatives for a more efficient and sustainable urban transport ecosystem.

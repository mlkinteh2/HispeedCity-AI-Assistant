better road networks infrastructure to improve traffic flow, however, driver’s

behavior somewhat contributes to the congestion; it may turn into vicious cycle

if lack of parking space therefore more expensive, enforcement officers

need to conduct regular crackdown on illegal parking, this can be done only on

certain hours or days/week.

The city need 24/7, 365days,

an e-enforcement O2O solution!

To increase awareness of self-discipline driving behavior, in line with the mission of transport department, we

shall make everyone responsible for the safety and discipline of all commuters. Back by traffic police

and authority, a punishment reward system to be introduced, there will be on the road e-enforcement

assistants patrolling and monitoring parking violation, and all registered users will be able to report

moment of illegal parking violation, offenders will receive penalty notification soon when a e-ticket is summoned,

fine will be deducted from deposit, reporters will be rewarded, all transactions on APP, paperless.

Offender of course, should learn the lesson and changing behavior to become “good citizen”, in return,

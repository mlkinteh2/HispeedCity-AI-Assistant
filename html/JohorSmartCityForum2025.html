Johor Smart City Forum 2025

Hispeedcity Smart Parking at Johor Smart City Forum 2025 (JSCF 2025) as Exhibitor

Johor Bahru, Johor – September 25, 2025 – Hispeedcity Sdn. Bhd., a pioneering Malaysian smart mobility solutions provider, was honoured to participate as one of the exhibitors at the Johor Smart City Forum 2025. The event, held at the Persada International Convention Centre, Johor Bahru (22–24 Sept 2025), under PLANMalaysia Johor, supports the Johor Smart City 2030 agenda.

The forum aligned stakeholders and showcased urban solutions across 80+ sessions, exhibitions, leadership forum, innovation awards and robotics competitions, drawing 15,000+ participants.

  • Smart Parking Off-Street — LPR-based seamless entry/exit.
  • Smart Parking On-Street — Sensor/QR for live availability and payments.

By mapping solutions to the Johor Smart City 2030 Blueprint, Hispeedcity is poised to scale deployments with councils unveiling action plans and ratings.

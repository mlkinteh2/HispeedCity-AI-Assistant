Hispeedcity at Taipei Smart City Summit & Expo 2026

Taipei, March 17, 2026 – Taiwan's Smart City Summit & Expo officially kicked off, bringing together global leaders in smart city innovation and digital transformation.

Hispeedcity proudly participated in this prestigious event, designed to highlight Taiwan's technological "soft power." The expo featured more than 2,250 booths and over 70 professional forums held across Taipei and Kaohsiung.

The event was organized by the Taipei Computer Association (TCA) and the National Development Council, attracting representatives from 174 cities across 53 countries, alongside more than 3,000 international visitors and over 250 startup teams.

We would like to express our deepest gratitude to the Taipei Computer Association (TCA) for organizing this outstanding event. We also extend our sincere thanks to Malaysia Digital Economy Corporation (MDEC) for their invitation and leadership of the Malaysian delegation.

The entire event was professionally structured, seamlessly executed, and thoughtfully designed for all participants. The interactive sessions were highly engaging and provided a strong platform for innovation, collaboration, and global partnerships.

During the event, Hispeedcity engaged with international delegates and city leaders, including representatives from São Paulo, Africa, Vietnam, and Belize. These interactions highlight the growing global collaboration in smart city innovation and digital mobility solutions.

Why it matters

The expo highlighted Hispeedcity participated in the 2024 Smart City Summit & Expo / 2050 Net Zero City Expo held in Taipei, where the company was given the opportunity to present its smart digital parking solution. The presentation was conducted together with MDEC and included an engagement session with the Taipei Minister of Transport. During the visit, Hispeedcity showcased its smart parking solution designed to support efficient and sustainable urban mobility across Taipei.

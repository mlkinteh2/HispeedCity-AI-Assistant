HISPEEDCITY Proposes Smart Toll MLFF to Ministry of Works

Advancing Malaysia’s Smart Tolling Vision

HISPEEDCITY was officially invited by the Ministry of Works Malaysia (Kementerian Kerja Raya, KKR) to present and demonstrate its integrated interoperability digital platform at the ministry’s headquarters in Kuala Lumpur.

This initiative supports Malaysia’s national digital transformation agenda to modernise more than 30 highways nationwide under the Multi-Lane Free Flow (MLFF) mega project. The vision is to fully eliminate physical toll plazas, card-based payments, and conventional RFID systems—enabling barrier-free, non-stop travel with automatic real-time deductions via e-wallets.

On 18 June 2025, during the Nikkei Forum held in Johor, Iskandar Puteri, HISPEEDCITY presented this transformative tolling model and received positive feedback during a briefing session with YAB Dato’ Sri Anwar Ibrahim, Prime Minister of Malaysia.

HISPEEDCITY remains actively engaged in advancing this national infrastructure mega project, driving seamless mobility, reducing congestion, and accelerating Malaysia’s digital highway transformation.

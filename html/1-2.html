When you create an Account, you will provide Information that could be Personal Information, such as the following:

Parking Space Owners

  • Email Address
  • Payment method or bank account information (processed via third parties – please see below for more information)

Parking Space Renters

  • Name

Personal Information also includes your log-in credentials, password, transactional information based upon your activity on the APP (information we need to get paid and to pay you), user feedback and comments, demographic information, and authentication information. We will track the parking transactions in real-time and have this data visible on our APP. ix.Information Related to Ads and the Use of Web Beacons:

  • ·Sometimes the ads are targeted to particular users, sometimes they are based on Personal Information (i.e. location, gender), Services usage patterns of particular users, or your activity on Third Party Services. Sometimes ads are anonymized.
  • ·Third party ads may enrich your user’s experience, so we may serve them up to you on the APP.
  • ·We like our ads to be effective, so we may deliver a file (known as a “web beacon”) from an ad network to you through the Services. Web beacons allow ad networks to deliver targeted ads and to provide anonymized, aggregated auditing, research and reporting for us and for advertisers, aka better data. Because your mobile or web browser must request these ads and web beacons from the ad network’s servers, these companies can view, edit or set their own cookies, just as if you had requested a web page from their site.
